**Description** (4000 max):

```
Greece's intercity coach network is dozens of independent KTEL operators, each with its own website, its own timetable format and its own terminal. Finding a coach is easy. Being certain of the right one is not.

Odivrelo answers four questions, and shows its working for each.

DOES IT RUN ON MY DATE?
Service dates are interpreted in Athens time, including journeys that leave before midnight and arrive after it. Calendar exceptions and daylight-saving changes are handled, not glossed over.

WHERE EXACTLY DO I BOARD?
Not the city. The terminal, and the bay. Athens alone has separate terminals serving opposite halves of the country, and arriving at the wrong one is a missed coach. Odivrelo shows the reviewed boarding point, its coordinates, and whether step-free access is known.

HOW DO I BUY A TICKET?
Odivrelo hands you to the operator's own booking page and returns you to exactly where you were. Where an operator does not sell online, you get its verified ticket office, address, phone and opening hours instead. Odivrelo never sells or issues tickets.

HOW STALE IS THIS?
Every fact carries when it was last checked, how old that is, which source supports it, and what rights that source was published under. If Odivrelo is not sure, it says so rather than guessing.

ALSO IN THE APP
• Offline packs with integrity checks, atomic installation, recovery from an interrupted download, update, rollback and deletion. The app tells you what is genuinely available offline instead of implying more.
• Saved trips and Trip Ready, usable without a connection, showing which cached release they came from.
• A private travel wallet for ticket files you import yourself. Stored on your device only. Never uploaded, never logged, never put in a shared cache.
• Opt-in reminders that never carry your name, your booking reference or your barcode.
• Greek, English and Albanian throughout.
• Full VoiceOver support, Dynamic Type, high contrast, reduced motion, and a complete non-map route to every piece of essential information.
• Adaptive layouts for iPhone, iPhone Duo and iPad, including multitasking and window resizing, with your search preserved across every change.
• No account. No analytics. No trackers.

ABOUT THIS BETA
This release ships demonstration data for a fictional region called Aloria, which does not exist. No departure, terminal, operator or fare in this build is real, and the app says so on every screen.

That is deliberate. Odivrelo's premise is that a missing journey is better than an invented one. The one openly licensed national dataset available today was last updated in 2020, contains no boarding points and no coordinates, and does not include the corridor we set out to verify. Rather than dress that up as coverage, this beta ships working software over data that announces what it is.

INDEPENDENCE
Odivrelo is an independent project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or operated by the KTEL federation, any regional KTEL operator, or any ticketing provider. Operator names and trademarks belong to their owners.
```
